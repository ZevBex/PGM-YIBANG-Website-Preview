# V9.42.15 — Clubhead Manufacturing Capability

Based on V9.42.14 (Tablet Banner Centering). This release makes clubhead development and production capability more visible without changing the existing site structure, navigation, homepage video, banner imagery, logo system, or unrelated pages.

- Homepage hero copy and capability line now explicitly include clubhead development and CLUBHEAD MANUFACTURING alongside PRODUCT DESIGN, CLUB ASSEMBLY and OEM / ODM.
- Homepage capability strip highlights CLUBHEAD MANUFACTURING, with supporting copy describing development and process coordination.
- Manufacturing page hero, overview and process section now explicitly present clubhead development and manufacturing routes.
- Manufacturing model names the specialist partner-supported scope as the Clubhead Manufacturing Network.
- English and Chinese content are aligned.

## Accuracy note
The wording distinguishes in-house engineering, assembly and QC from selected specialist manufacturing processes coordinated through long-term partner factories. It does not claim that every casting, forging, machining, joining or finishing operation is performed in-house. Process allocation remains project-specific.
