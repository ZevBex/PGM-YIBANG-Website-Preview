RULES OF GOLF · MODEL-SPECIFIC REVIEW

Competition conformity should be verified by exact model.

USGA / R&A conformity is not a blanket claim for an entire brand or product range. For driver heads, check the exact model, loft and identification markings against the current Conforming Driver Heads List. Other clubs must meet the applicable Equipment Rules. Final configuration and any subsequent modification also matter.

USGA Conforming Driver Heads List ↗ The R&A Equipment Rules ↗
01 / DRIVER HEADSVerify model name, loft and markings on the current list.
02 / ALL CLUB TYPESReview the applicable club requirements in the Equipment Rules.
03 / FINAL CONFIGURATIONConfirm the approved specification and assess any later changes separately.

This page does not state that every PGM model is approved or listed. Confirm the exact model and current documentation before making a competition-conformity claim.
