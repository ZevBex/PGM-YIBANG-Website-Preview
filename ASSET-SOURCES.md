## Important usage and publishing notes

- The Sina Sports photographs are third-party press images. Public availability does not grant commercial website or advertising rights. Obtain written permission or replace them with PGM-approved originals before public commercial launch.
- PGM store product images should also be used in accordance with PGM's image-use permissions and brand guidelines.
- Player names, likenesses, signatures, endorsement statements and collaboration marks must match the current agreements and approved territories / media.
- The TIG039 image is only a representative PGM golf club product visual. It must not be represented as the CHONG Series model. Replace it with a specific CHONG Series product photograph when an approved one is available.
- The factory video is a PGM-published introduction. It should not be presented as proof of every current process or facility condition.
- For stable deployment, obtain approved high-resolution originals and host them in `assets/images/` in this repository rather than relying on external hotlinks.
