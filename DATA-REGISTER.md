## Source hierarchy
1. Latest dated China Golf Association publication with explicit reporting period.
2. Latest dated PGM official annual or campaign report.
3. Current official PGM pages for current partner-network or brand statements.
4. Platform analytics, award certificates and signed event agreements when supplied by the company.

## Publication controls
- State the platform, category, geography, date range and source for every ranking.
- Separate campaign-period growth from annual growth.
- Separate PGM brand-level scale from YIBANG's actual in-house manufacturing scope.
- Confirm ISO certificate holder, standard, site scope, issuing body and validity before public display.
- Confirm R&A / USGA records by exact model and document version.
- Confirm current rights for player names, portraits, signatures, event photos and logos.
