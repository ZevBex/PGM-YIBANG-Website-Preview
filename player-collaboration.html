Feng Shanshan

LPGA Major Champion · Olympic Bronze Medalist · Former World No. 1

Feng made history as the first player from mainland China to win an LPGA major in 2012. She won the bronze medal at the Rio 2016 Olympic Games and reached No. 1 in the Rolex Women's World Golf Rankings in 2017.
