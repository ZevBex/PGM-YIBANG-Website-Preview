# Professional Player Collaboration — V9.16 Website Scope

The current website presents professional player relationships as part of PGM's brand credibility and focuses on golf-club-related collaboration stories.

- Feng Shanshan — PGM's public materials include golf-club campaign participation. The page links to the PGM club ambassador story.

Player profile photographs and all name, likeness, campaign and product endorsement usage must be confirmed against current written permissions before commercial publication.
