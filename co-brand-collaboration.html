STRATEGIC BRAND COLLABORATION

More than a logo.
A shared product vision.

PGM Co-Brand Collaboration is a selective, jointly planned product-development partnership. We start with a shared product concept, define each party’s contribution and target market, then align product development, brand presentation and commercial responsibilities under a written agreement.

THE CO-BRAND PARTNERSHIP MODEL

One shared vision.
Clearly defined roles.

A meaningful co-brand project combines more than two logos. Each proposal is reviewed for brand fit, product direction, development responsibilities and a practical route to market.

01 / PRODUCT

Shared Product Direction

Define the intended golfer, club category, product concept and contribution from each party before development begins.

02 / DEVELOPMENT

Coordinated Development

Review design and engineering needs, component options, sample feedback and appropriate production arrangements for the agreed scope.

03 / BRAND & MARKET

Authorized Brand Expression

Agree how names, trademarks, packaging, market scope and launch materials may be used — with the rights documented in writing.

WHO THIS ROUTE IS FOR

Partners with something meaningful to contribute.

Established golf brands, qualified professional partners, golf clubs or organizations with a clear audience and an original collaboration proposal may be considered.

WHAT TO SHARE WITH US

Start with a focused collaboration brief.

  • Brand or organization profile and target audience
  • Proposed golf club concept and target market
  • Expected partner contribution and proposed brand usage

Every collaboration proposal is subject to PGM review, product feasibility, brand-rights clearance and a separate written agreement. Approval is not automatic.

HOW A NEW CO-BRAND PROJECT STARTS

From proposal to approved partnership.

01

Partner & brand review

Understand the partner profile, audience, brand fit and collaboration objective.

02

Joint product brief

Define the product category, customer, market and proposed contribution from each party.

03

Development & approval

Review product samples, brand presentation, names, marks and promotional materials.

04

Agreement & launch plan

Document rights, responsibilities, market scope, commercial terms and launch approvals.
