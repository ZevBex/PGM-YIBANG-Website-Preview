WHEN YOUR PROJECT NEEDS MORE THAN PRIVATE LABEL

Beyond private label.
Develop around your brief.

OEM / ODM is for projects that need new product structure, design or engineering — not only a logo change. Start with a customer drawing, physical reference sample or early-stage concept. We help assess the brief and define the design, tooling, sample, production route and quality checkpoints before an order moves forward.

OEM OR ODM?

Two ways to start.
One coordinated development route.

Choose the route that best matches the information you already have. The final scope, responsibilities and deliverables are confirmed project by project.

OEM / CUSTOMER-LED

Develop to your specification

You bring a drawing, technical brief or reference sample. We review the requirements, engineering details and production route, then coordinate development against the agreed specification.

A GOOD FIT FOR: DRAWINGS · SPECIFICATIONS · REFERENCE SAMPLES

ODM / CO-DEVELOPMENT

Develop from an idea

You bring a product concept, target market or design direction. We collaborate on product structure, 2D / 3D engineering and sample refinement to shape the development brief.

A GOOD FIT FOR: PRODUCT IDEAS · MARKET NEEDS · DESIGN DIRECTION

PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT CAPABILITIES

The capabilities behind your project.
Design. Develop. Coordinate. Assemble. Inspect.

Our work combines in-house product development, club assembly and QC with a long-term specialist manufacturing network for selected processes.

01 / IN-HOUSE DEVELOPMENT

Product concept & design review

Review sketches, customer drawings and physical references. Align product direction, target requirements and design priorities before detailed development.

02 / ENGINEERING

2D / 3D engineering & structure

Support 2D drawings, 3D CAD modeling and product-structure review according to the approved project brief. Reference-sample analysis can be included where required.

03 / TOOLING & SAMPLES

Tooling & sample coordination

Review tooling requirements and coordinate prototype or sample development. Tooling scope, ownership, costs and sample schedule are confirmed for each project.

04 / PARTNER NETWORK · AS REQUIRED

Specialist component processing

Coordinate selected casting, forging, CNC machining, joining and surface-finishing processes through long-term specialist partners where required.

05 / IN-HOUSE ASSEMBLY

Club assembly & specification matching

Our in-house assembly team coordinates component preparation, shaft and grip installation, length and swing-weight adjustment, and set matching as required by the agreed specification.

06 / IN-HOUSE QC

Quality control & inspection

Our in-house QC function reviews agreed dimensions, weight, configuration, appearance, assembly and pre-shipment requirements, as applicable to the product.

WHAT TO SHARE WITH OUR TEAM

A useful brief
is enough to begin.

No complete drawing yet? A concept or physical reference sample is enough for an initial discussion.

  • Product drawing, sketch or reference sample
  • Target golfer, market and product category
  • Key performance, structure and appearance requirements
  • Preferred materials or components, if known
  • Estimated quantity and target timeline
  • Any confidentiality or NDA requirements

For confidential drawings or samples, mention this in your brief. We can discuss an NDA and a suitable file-sharing method.
