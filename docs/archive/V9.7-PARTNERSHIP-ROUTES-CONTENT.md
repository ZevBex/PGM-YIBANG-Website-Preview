# Partnership Routes — Website Content

1. **PGM Brand Distribution** — for importers, distributors, retailers and pro shops. Appointment, territory and channel rights require review and written agreement.
2. **Existing-Design Private Label** — select existing golf club designs and apply the buyer's own brand identity, with model-specific options confirmed per project.
3. **Fully Customized OEM / ODM** — develop from drawings, physical samples or product concepts; 2D / 3D design, tooling where required, sample development and revisions are subject to feasibility and agreed scope.
4. **PGM Co-Brand Collaboration** — joint product and brand collaboration for qualified partners, subject to PGM brand review, rights clearance, product approval and a separate written agreement.

The four routes are intentionally distinguished by brand ownership, product starting point, development depth and approval requirements.
