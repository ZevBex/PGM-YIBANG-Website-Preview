# V9.8 — Global Navigation Experience

## Navigation architecture
The site now uses six primary navigation pillars:
1. Home
2. Products
3. Partnerships
4. Manufacturing
5. Brand & Trust
6. Resources

The Get a Quote action remains a separate highlighted CTA and opens the Smart RFQ Builder.

## Reorganized routes
- Club Configurator moved under Products.
- Digital Factory Tour moved under Manufacturing.
- Smart RFQ Builder is reached through Get a Quote.
- OEM / ODM details remain available under Partnerships.
- Athlete, association, national-team and CHONG Series stories are grouped under Brand & Trust.
- Buyer Guide, Insights, FAQs and Contact are grouped under Resources.

## Interaction and responsive behavior
- Mega menus on desktop with grouped links and concise descriptions.
- Only one dropdown can be open at a time.
- Outside click and Escape close open menus.
- Current section and current page are highlighted.
- Mobile and tablet navigation switches to an accordion-style full-screen panel.
- Language switch and Get a Quote CTA remain available in the mobile panel.
- Existing pages, product data, forms, configurator, factory tour, partnership content and footer links are retained.

## Notes
The site has no standalone downloadable resource library page or public brochure archive in this build. Resources therefore links to existing Buyer Guide, Insights, FAQ and Contact routes, with a product-catalogue request routed to the Contact page rather than presenting a non-existent download centre.
