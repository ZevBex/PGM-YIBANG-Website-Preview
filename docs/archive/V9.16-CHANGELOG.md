# V9.16 — Site Structure & Content Consolidation

## Navigation and global shell
- Replaced the large repeated mega menus with a compact six-pillar navigation: Golf Clubs, Partnerships, Manufacturing, Brand & Trust, Resources and Contact.
- Simplified the footer into five focused columns.
- Added consistent V9.16 site styling and responsive dropdown behavior across the 22 HTML pages.

## Content architecture
- Home: reduced repeated detailed modules to concise section previews and links.
- Products: removed the golf-bag partnership strip and training-aid product section; the product center is golf clubs only.
- Partnership Solutions: remains the canonical page for the four business cooperation models.
- OEM / ODM: removed the repeated four-model comparison and focuses on technical golf-club customization and development.
- Manufacturing: remains the canonical technical capabilities page; added material selection and a concise QC overview.
- Factory: focused on company-provided workshop photographs, guided factory photo tour and factory video.
- Quality: remains the detailed inspection equipment and documentation page.
- Capabilities and Factory Tour: retained as legacy redirects to the canonical pages.
- Brand Heritage: owns PGM history, official CGA / national-team partnership evidence and documentary brand records.
- Player Collaboration: focused on the professional player stories; institutional partnerships and the full CHONG case are linked to their canonical pages.
- Market Impact: keeps market performance and event coverage; removes repeated national-team partnership feature.
- Insights: becomes a technical-topic hub; Buyer Guide remains the single sourcing checklist.
- Contact: simplified to a short enquiry form; Project Builder retains the detailed RFQ.

## Product scope
- Product navigation, product catalogue, contact interest and Project Builder choices are limited to golf-club categories.
- Non-golf-club product categories are not offered as product navigation or RFQ categories.

## Repetition reduction
- Official partnership photographs are shown in full on Brand Heritage; homepage uses a text-only preview.
- Manufacturing client logos are displayed on Manufacturing only; Brand Heritage links to that section.
- Detailed QC equipment photographs are displayed on Quality only; Manufacturing links to Quality.
- Removed repeated institutional partnership content from Player Collaboration and Market Impact.
- Removed repeated model-comparison content from OEM / ODM.
- Removed duplicate real production photos from Manufacturing where the Factory page is the canonical visual location.

## Domain and deployment
- Removed the old `mypgmgolf.com` sitemap and robots reference.
- Removed the old absolute `_next` redirect from the RFQ form.
