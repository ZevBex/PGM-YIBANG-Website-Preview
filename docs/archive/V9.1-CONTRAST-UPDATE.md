# PGM × YIBANG GOLF — V9.1 Contrast & Readability Update

V9.1 is a focused visual-accessibility refinement of V9.0. It keeps the Future Golf Technology design language, page structure, product content and interactive functions. The change targets text that was too close in tone to its background, especially small supporting labels and metadata.

## What changed

- Added `assets/css/contrast-optimized.css` and loaded it after the existing theme styles on all 20 HTML pages.
- Darkened small gold labels on pale backgrounds while retaining champagne-gold accents on dark navy surfaces.
- Increased contrast for helper copy, step descriptions, product metadata, form progress labels, factory-tour tab labels and configurator preview notes.
- Increased legibility of the homepage proof strip, factory notes, video metadata and footer legal line.
- Added a subtle dark backing to the hero image credit so it remains readable over photography.
- Improved form-field borders, option text and keyboard focus indicators.
- Added a `prefers-contrast: more` refinement for users who request higher contrast.
- Kept dark surfaces dark and the cyan / champagne-gold technology palette intact.

## Contrast targets

The revised text colors are selected to meet WCAG AA contrast for normal text (4.5:1) on the intended flat light and dark surfaces wherever a solid background is known. Examples:

| Foreground | Background | Contrast |
|---|---|---:|
| `#526575` | `#F5F7F9` | 5.62:1 |
| `#76551F` | `#F5F7F9` | 6.33:1 |
| `#805D25` | `#FFFFFF` | 6.00:1 |
| `#C3D1DB` | `#071522` | 11.82:1 |
| `#E5CF9C` | `#071522` | 12.06:1 |

Photographic backgrounds, gradients and video frames can vary by image and screen. The hero image credit now uses a dark translucent backing, but the final production site should still be visually checked on desktop and mobile.

## Files changed

- `assets/css/contrast-optimized.css` — new global contrast refinement layer.
- All 20 HTML pages — added the stylesheet after `v9-experience.css`.
- `README.md` and `V9.0-CHANGELOG.md` — updated version and deployment notes.

## No functional changes

The configurator, factory tour, RFQ builder, product pages, language switcher, videos, FormSubmit endpoint, WhatsApp link and GitHub Pages paths are unchanged.
