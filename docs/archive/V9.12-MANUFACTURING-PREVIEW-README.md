# V9.12 Manufacturing Page — Local Design Draft

This package contains the complete V9.11 website source plus a standalone `manufacturing.html` page with integrated visual concepts. The original factory-tour experience, factory video, audit request, and legacy page content are retained. Shared Manufacturing navigation and footer links now point buyers to the integrated engineering and manufacturing page while preserving the guided factory explorer.

## Preview locally
1. Extract the ZIP to a folder on your computer.
2. Open `manufacturing.html` in Chrome or Edge.
3. Use the EN / 中文 switch in the top navigation.
4. Test the manufacturing process filters and click QC / assembly photos to enlarge them.

No new domain is configured for this draft. It is not deployed and does not overwrite the existing website. The original V9.11 sitemap / robots and other legacy site settings are retained in this preview copy; do not deploy this ZIP as-is. Before launch, update canonical URLs, sitemap, robots, form return URLs and any domain references for the new independent domain.

## Page scope
- Golf-club-only manufacturing and engineering content.
- Company-provided photographs are reused from `assets/images/owner-provided/` as evidence of actual engineering, production, customization and QC resources.
- Eight AI-generated images are stored in `assets/images/illustrative/` and explicitly labeled on the page as illustrative visuals, not real factory, employee or product photographs.
- Actual company-provided process and QC photographs remain visible alongside the illustrative visuals; individual equipment ownership and process allocation are not assumed.
- The page distinguishes in-house engineering, assembly and QC from selected partner-supported specialist processing.
- No unverified production capacity, tolerance, pass-rate, certification or delivery-time figures are added.

## Visual-source policy
- AI-generated visuals are for design atmosphere and explanatory illustration only. They must not be described as actual factory photographs, company personnel, owned equipment or completed PGM products.
- Use the company-provided images in `assets/images/owner-provided/` when presenting real workshops, equipment, production references and QC resources.
- This remains a local preview package. The existing production-domain canonical URLs and sitemap have not been changed because the new independent domain has not yet been configured.
