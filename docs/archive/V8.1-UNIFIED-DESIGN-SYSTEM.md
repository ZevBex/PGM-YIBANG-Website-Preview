# V8.1 — Unified Future Golf Technology Design System

## Scope
V8.1 extends the V8.0 Future Golf Technology visual language from the homepage to all 15 remaining HTML pages. The existing page information architecture, product catalogue data, bilingual content, enquiry form, WhatsApp contact, brand / athlete content, and supplied video URLs are retained.

## Shared design language
- Midnight navy and near-black foundations, restrained champagne-gold accents, and limited technical cyan details.
- Glass-metal navigation, consistent typography, rounded technical controls, subtle borders, and controlled light effects.
- Cinematic page heroes with page-specific photographic atmosphere.
- Alternating dark surfaces and glass-style cards to create a continuous premium technology experience without making every section identical.
- Unified button, product card, data table, form, video, image gallery, footer and responsive treatments.

## Page-level experience
- All inner pages share the compact glass navigation, active-page state, scroll progress rail, persistent quote action and back-to-top control.
- Main content headings and selected content cards reveal as they enter the viewport.
- Selected image and capability cards use restrained pointer tilt on desktop only.
- Each major content section receives a small numbered PGM × YIBANG marker.
- Reduced-motion preferences are respected; mobile navigation and page layouts remain responsive.
- Existing product filters, product-detail rendering, enquiry form prefill, bilingual switch, video controls and WhatsApp links are preserved.

## Page-specific atmosphere
The hero visual treatment is tailored by page purpose: product / insights pages use golf-club visuals; capabilities and buyer-guide pages use design-development imagery; factory and quality pages use company-supplied production / QC imagery; OEM / ODM uses customization imagery; brand / market pages use the showroom visual language; contact and privacy pages retain restrained brand context.

## Accuracy and launch notes
- Company-provided production, QC, design and showroom photographs remain the source material for relevant factory pages. Some specialist processing is still described as coordinated through long-term partner factories, not claimed as entirely in-house.
- AI-generated driver artwork remains a concept visual, not a confirmed PGM product model.
- Temporary third-party athlete portraits and any imagery requiring rights clearance must be replaced with PGM-authorized assets before commercial launch.
- The configurator remains a visual enquiry concept. It does not confirm technical feasibility, production availability, pricing or final specifications.
- Public credentials, player product categories, statistics, certification scope and commercial rights remain subject to the applicable source documents and agreements.

## Deployment
1. Back up the current GitHub repository.
2. Unzip the V8.1 package.
3. Upload the contents of the `PGM-YIBANG-Golf-Website-V8.1-Future-Golf-Technology-Bilingual/` folder to the repository root, preserving `assets/` and all relative paths.
4. Test the EN / 中文 switch, desktop and mobile navigation, product filters, product detail pages, configurator, factory explorer, videos, enquiry form and WhatsApp.
5. Confirm FormSubmit activation and commercial image / likeness rights before public campaigns.
