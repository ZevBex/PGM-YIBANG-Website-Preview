# V9.20 — Hero, Navigation & Product System Fix

## Visual hierarchy
- Rebalanced hero heights by page type: brand/editorial, image-led category, utility, and product-detail pages.
- Rebuilt the V9.18 image-led hero DOM so text and image occupy explicit columns rather than relying on CSS grid auto-placement.
- Reduced the product-detail title band so the product gallery appears much earlier in the first viewport.

## Navigation
- Restored the original light dropdown-card appearance on desktop while keeping the current anchored positioning and mobile two-level navigation.
- Mobile submenu links now use a clean light-card treatment without requiring a long scroll through the full menu.

## Golf club product system
- All six visual category cards are now full-card links to the relevant filtered product list.
- Individual product cards remain linked to their model/series detail page.
- Category filters update the visible cards and preserve the selected category in the URL.
- Unified product card proportions and added clear hover/focus states.
- Enlarged the product-detail gallery and made the main image full-width within its panel using contain behavior to avoid cropping the club.

## Rules and conformity communication
- Added a model-specific USGA / R&A conformity information section to the product detail template.
- Added direct links to the USGA Conforming Driver Heads List and The R&A Equipment Rules.
- Explicitly avoided a blanket “USGA / R&A approved” claim; exact model, loft, markings and final configuration must be verified.

## Brand ambassadors
- Added career highlights to the homepage ambassador cards, placed at the lower image-safe area rather than over faces.
- Highlights: Shanshan Feng — LPGA major champion, 23 weeks at World No.1, Olympic bronze; Liang Wenchong — 2007 Asian Tour Order of Merit winner, two Asian Tour wins, 2010 PGA Championship T8; Wu Ashun — five DP World Tour wins and two Volvo China Open titles.
- Replaced cross-category ambassador role labels with the consistent “PGM Brand Ambassador” label to keep the golf-club website focused.

## Verification notes
- Achievement wording is based on official LPGA, DP World Tour and Asian Tour sources.
- Player portrait commercial-use permissions remain subject to confirmation.
- The conformity section explains the verification process and does not claim that every PGM model is listed or approved.
